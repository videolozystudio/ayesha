About the Birthday Star

Ayesha is a remarkable poet who expresses her deepest emotions through Poetry, Nazm, Sher, and Ghazal. There is a unique tranquility in her beautiful words. Beyond her poetic talent, she also plays the flute, and the sweet melody of her music makes the atmosphere even more enchanting.
